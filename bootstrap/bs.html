The UEFA Champions League

The UEFA Champions League is the most prestigious club football tournament in Europe. Organized by UEFA, the competition brings together the top football clubs from different European countries to compete for the title of European champions.

About UCL History

The UEFA Champions League was created in 1955 and was originally known as the European Cup. The first tournament was won by Real Madrid, which dominated the early years by winning the first five titles.
